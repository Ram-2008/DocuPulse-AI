PARTIES:

1. PURPOSE
The parties wish to explore a potential strategic technology integration and business collaboration concerning cloud security automation (the "Purpose").

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" means all non-public, proprietary information disclosed by either party, including trade secrets, software code, customer data, and system architectures.

This Agreement shall remain in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date (expiring February 1, 2028). Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days prior written notice. The obligations of confidentiality shall survive for three (3) years post termination.
